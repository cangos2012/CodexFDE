## 图 1

Create a polished Chinese educational infographic, landscape 16:9, high resolution, white background, navy text, blue/teal/orange accents, crisp large simplified Chinese typography, generous spacing, no decoration, no tiny text. Title exact: “一个需求，怎样变成软件？” Subtitle: “例子：仓库人员需要导出库存表”. Draw 9 numbered cards in three rows of three, clear continuous snake arrows 1→2→3 then down to 4 on right, 4→5→6 moving right to left, then down to 7 on left, 7→8→9 rightward. Each card contains heading, responsible people and concrete meaning. Second row RIGHT TO LEFT: 4 “技术方案” / “开发人员” / “确定数据来源和实现办法”; 5 “技术评审” / “技术负责人 + 开发” / “检查可行性与风险”; 6 “开发” / “开发人员 + AI” / “按约定实现导出”. Third row left to right: 7 “测试” / “测试或复验人员” / “运行功能，检查结果”; 8 “业务方验收” / “业务方” / “确认这张表能用于工作”; 9 “上线” / “获授权的发布人员” / “部署到正式使用环境”.
